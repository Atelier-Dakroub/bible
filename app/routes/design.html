The reading room

The book for the text, the atlas for maps and time, the museum for cards and labels. Use the settings below: each one changes the whole page, as it will change the table.

On the table

Cards are paper with a tab in the pigment of their kind. The text has an initial in lapis at a chapter's start, notes in the margin from the cross-references, and the words of Christ in vermilion, the rubric.

The atlas

In the manner of a good atlas: paper land tinted by height, with a contour at each step (200, 500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,000 and 3,000 m) and a hill shade lit from the northwest; a pale, flat sea; water named in italic capitals; places in verdigris. Time and charts use the same pigments.

The pigments

Each color with its APCA contrast on the paper of the setting now chosen. Text needs Lc 75 for the Bible text, Lc 60 for a label; a line needs Lc 15.

Type

Literata, a book serif with optical sizes, for everything that is read; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a sans designed for legibility, for everything that is used.
