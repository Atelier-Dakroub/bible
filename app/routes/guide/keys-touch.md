# Keys and touch

This page lists the keyboard shortcuts and the touch gestures, and tells you how the table works on each kind of screen.

On a Mac, use <kbd>Command</kbd> where this page says <kbd>Command</kbd> or <kbd>Control</kbd>. On other computers, use <kbd>Control</kbd>.

## Keyboard shortcuts

### The table

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>/</kbd> | Opens the search panel and puts the cursor in the field |
| Arrow keys, when nothing has the focus | Pan the table |
| <kbd>O</kbd>, when nothing has the focus | Shows the whole table, small (the overview), or goes back |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd>, in the overview | Goes back to the zoom before the overview |
| <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd>, when nothing has the focus | Zoom the table in and out a step |
| <kbd>0</kbd>, when nothing has the focus | Zooms the table to full size |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> and an arrow key | Pans the table further |
| <kbd>Command</kbd> or <kbd>Control</kbd> and <kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd>, <kbd>Command</kbd> or <kbd>Control</kbd>, and <kbd>Z</kbd> | Redo |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Moves the focus to the next control or card |

### The rail and the panels

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys, on the rail | Move along the buttons of the rail. The rail is one stop for <kbd>Tab</kbd>. |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd>, on the rail | Go to the first and the last button |
| Arrow keys, on the grip of a panel | Move the panel. A docked panel floats first. |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> and an arrow key, on a grip | Moves the panel further |

### A card

First give the card the focus: press <kbd>Tab</kbd> until the card has the focus, or click it.

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys | Move the card |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> and an arrow key | Moves the card further |
| <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd> | Make the card larger or smaller |
| <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd> | Turn the card |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Does what a tap on the heading does. On a passage, opens its guide. |
| <kbd>Delete</kbd> or <kbd>Backspace</kbd> | Takes the card off the table. Undo brings it back. |

### The map

First give the frame of the map the focus with <kbd>Tab</kbd>.

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys | Move the map |
| <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd> | Zoom in and out |
| <kbd>0</kbd> | Shows the whole map |

### The search panel

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd>, in the field | Searches |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd>, in the field | Hides the app's keyboard, then folds the results away, then closes the panel |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>, on a result | Puts the result on the table |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> | Goes back on the research trail |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Goes forward on the research trail |

### The timeline

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys | Move from tick to tick, or from span to span |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> | Go to the first and the last tick or span |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Shows the tick or span in the search panel |

### The Tools panel

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys, on the tabs | Move from tab to tab |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Moves from group to group of choices |
| Arrow keys, in a group | Choose within the group |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd> | Closes a docked panel |

### Notes

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd> | Stops the writing |
| <kbd>Command</kbd> or <kbd>Control</kbd> and <kbd>B</kbd> | Bold |
| <kbd>Command</kbd> or <kbd>Control</kbd> and <kbd>I</kbd> | Italic |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd>, in a list | Move an item in and out |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>F10</kbd> (<kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>F10</kbd> on a Mac), with words selected | Go into the format bar; <kbd>Escape</kbd> goes back |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd>, after a reference that you typed | Makes it a reference |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd>, with suggestions | Choose a suggestion |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Tab</kbd>, with @ suggestions | Makes the suggestion a reference |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd>, in a title | Keeps the title |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd>, in a title | Keeps the title that was there before |

See [Notes](/guide/notes#keys-for-formatting) for the keys that make headings and lists.

### Videos

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>K</kbd> | Plays or pauses |
| <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> | Go back or forward 10 seconds |
| <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd>, on the position bar | Go back or forward 5 seconds |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd> | Leaves the full screen on an iPhone |

## Touch, mouse and trackpad

| To | Touch | Mouse or trackpad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pan the table | Drag the empty table | Drag the empty table, or scroll |
| Zoom the table in or out | Pinch with two fingers, on the table or on a card | Pinch on the trackpad, or <kbd>Control</kbd> or <kbd>Command</kbd> and the wheel, or the **−** and **+** buttons in the corner |
| Zoom back to full size | Tap the percentage in the corner | Click the percentage, or press <kbd>0</kbd> |
| Show the whole table (the overview) | Pinch in past 25% | Pinch in past 25%, or press <kbd>O</kbd> |
| Go to a card or a place in the overview | Tap it | Click it |
| Move a card | Drag it with one finger | Drag it |
| Move a card freely, with no snap | Turn off **Snap** in **Tools**, **Table** | Hold <kbd>Command</kbd> or <kbd>Control</kbd> while you drag |
| Select words to copy | Double-tap the words, then select them | Double-click the words, then drag over them |
| Mark words | Hold the card with one finger, run a finger of the other hand over the words | Hold <kbd>Option</kbd> (<kbd>Alt</kbd>) and drag over the words |
| Draw a link | Mark words, then drag the marking finger onto another card | The same, with <kbd>Option</kbd> held |
| Make a phrase card | Mark words, then lift the marking finger on the empty table | The same, with <kbd>Option</kbd> held |
| Remove a card | Tap **×** | Click **×** |

You cannot mark words with the keyboard alone.

## Kinds of screen

The app chooses a size for the kind of screen. To make everything smaller or larger on this device, use **Interface** in **Tools**, **Table**.

### A phone

- The rail is along the bottom of the screen, with icons only.
- Each panel is the width of the screen, and one panel opens at a time. A panel cannot float.
- A card from the search panel goes on the table at the width of the screen. The search panel steps aside so that you can see the card.
- The timeline is closed at first.
- A pinch zooms the table, not the page. On the map, a pinch zooms the map.

### A tablet

The gestures are the same as on a large touch panel. The tablet uses its own keyboard, so the app's keyboard does not show.

### A laptop or a desktop

Use the mouse, the trackpad and the keys in the tables above.

### A large touch panel

- Rest your palm on the table when you want. The table does not act on a resting hand.
- When a finger touches a field and no keyboard is near, the app's own keyboard shows.
- On a large screen, such as a 4K panel, the app makes the interface larger, for reading at arm's length.

### The wall

Set **Interface** to **Far** in **Tools**, **Table**. Everything becomes twice the size of **M**, for a wall that you see from across the room. The arrow keys of a remote, or of a keyboard, now move the focus:

- The arrow keys move the focus to the nearest card or panel control in that direction. A ring shows where the focus is.
- When nothing has the focus, the first arrow key goes to the card nearest the middle of the screen.
- The table pans to show a card that is off the screen.
- <kbd>Enter</kbd> on a card opens its guide.
- <kbd>Enter</kbd> on the map card steps into the map. Then the arrow keys and <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd> move it. <kbd>Escape</kbd> steps out to the card.

> If a size is too large to reach the settings, open [the table at size M](/table?ui=m).

## The app's on-screen keyboard

The app has its own keyboard for a large touch screen with no keyboard near. It shows when a finger touches a field, such as the search field, the name of a table or a note.

It shows only for a finger, not for a mouse or a pen. At the **Automatic** setting, it also does not show:

- On a phone or a tablet, which have their own keyboards.
- After you press a key of a real keyboard during the visit.

To hide it, tap its **Hide** key, press <kbd>Escape</kbd>, or tap away from the field. When you write a note, **Hide** hides only the keyboard, and the note stays open.

The keyboard has the letters, the digits, **⇧** (Shift) for capitals, **⌫** to delete the last letter, and **Clear** to empty the field. The key at the bottom right does the field's action: **Search**, **Save** or **New line**.

To choose when the keyboard shows, use **On-screen keyboard** in **Tools**, **Table**: **Automatic**, **Always** or **Never**.
