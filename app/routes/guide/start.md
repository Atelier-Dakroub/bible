# Start here

This page tells you about the table and the rail, how to put a passage down, and how to move the cards and the table.

## The table

The table is a surface with no edge. Everything you put on it is a card: a passage, a person, a place, the map, a picture, a video or a note.

The browser keeps the table for you. When you open the table again, the same cards are in the same places. To keep a table under a name, or in a file, see [Tools](/guide/tools#saved).

## The rail

The rail is the strip of tools on one edge of the screen. At first it is on the left. It has these buttons, in this order:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Opens or closes the search panel. |
| **Timeline** | Opens or closes the timeline. |
| **Map** | Puts the map card on the table, or takes it away. |
| **Books** | Puts the card of the 66 books on the table, or takes it away. |
| **Note** | Puts a new note on the table and opens it for writing. |
| **Undo** | Takes back the last change to the table. |
| **Redo** | Does again the change that Undo took back. |
| **Tools** | Opens or closes the Tools panel. |
| **Hide** | Clears the screen of the rail and the panels. |

A button is lapis blue while its panel is open, or while its card is on the table.

### Move the rail

- Drag the grip (⋮⋮) at the end of the rail to an edge of the screen.
- Or tap the grip. The rail goes to the next edge.
- Or choose the edge in **Tools**, **Table**, **Rail on the**.

### Move a panel

The search panel, the timeline and the Tools panel open beside the rail. This is "docked".

- Drag the grip of a panel off the rail. The panel floats where you lift your finger.
- Drag it back onto the rail to dock it again.
- Or tap the float button at the top of the panel.

A floating panel stays open until you close it. A docked Tools panel closes when you tap outside it.

> On a phone, the rail is along the bottom and shows icons only. One panel opens at a time, and a panel cannot float.

### Hide the tools

Tap **Hide**. The rail and the panels go away, and one small Show button stays at the edge. Tap it to show the tools again.

## Put a passage down

1. Tap **Search** on the rail. Or press <kbd>/</kbd> on a keyboard.
2. Type a reference, such as `John 3:16` or `Gen 1:1-2:3`, or some words.
3. Put a result on the table in one of these ways:
  - Tap the result. The card goes into the open part of the table.
  - Drag the result out to the side. The card comes out under your finger, and you can put it where you want.
  - With the keyboard, move to the result with <kbd>Tab</kbd>, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>.

A drag up or down in the results scrolls the list. Only a drag to the side pulls a card out.

A passage of more than 24 verses comes onto the table at half size, in its short form. See [Cards](/guide/cards#size-and-the-short-form).

## Move, turn and scale a card

| To | Touch | Mouse or trackpad | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move a card | Drag it with one finger | Drag it | Move to it with <kbd>Tab</kbd>, then press the arrow keys |
| Scale a card | Pinch it with two fingers | Pinch on the trackpad, or hold <kbd>Control</kbd> or <kbd>Command</kbd> and turn the wheel | <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd> |
| Turn a card | Turn two fingers on it | Turn two fingers on the trackpad (Safari only) | <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd> |

Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> with the arrow keys to move a card further.

### Snapping

When you hold a card and let it go, it snaps:

- It turns upright when it is near upright, or near a quarter turn.
- It goes to full size when it is near full size.
- It lines up with the edges and middles of the other upright cards, or one gap beside them. Dotted guides show where it lines up.

To move a card freely with a mouse, hold <kbd>Command</kbd> or <kbd>Control</kbd> while you drag. To stop all snapping, turn off **Snap** in **Tools**, **Table**. The wheel and the keys do not snap.

## Pan the table

| Touch | Mouse or trackpad | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drag the empty table | Drag the empty table, or scroll | The arrow keys, when no card has the focus |

Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> with the arrow keys to pan further. The table does not scale. To see more, make the cards smaller.

A tap on the empty table also folds the search results away. Tap the search field to see them again.

## Remove a card

- Tap the **×** at the top of the card.
- Or give the card the focus and press <kbd>Delete</kbd> or <kbd>Backspace</kbd>.

## Undo and Redo

**Undo** and **Redo** on the rail step back and forward through every change to the table. A pan of the table is not a change. A move of the map inside its frame, and a step on the research trail, are not changes either.

On a keyboard, press <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> (on a Mac) or <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> to undo. Add <kbd>Shift</kbd> to redo.

> When you write in a note or a field, these keys undo your typing. The table takes the whole edit of a note as one step, after you finish the note.

## A resting hand

On a large touch screen, you can rest your palm on the table. The table finds a group of touches close together and does not act on them.
