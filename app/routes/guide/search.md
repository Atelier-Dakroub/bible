# Search

This page tells you what the search field understands, how to use the results, and how the research trail, "Did you know?" and the questions list work.

## Open the search panel

- Tap **Search** on the rail.
- Or press <kbd>/</kbd> anywhere on the table. The cursor goes into the field.

The panel grows as you use it. At first it is only the field. When an answer comes, the trail and the results show under the field. The results come as you type. You can also press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or tap **Search**.

- A tap on the empty table folds the results away. Tap the field to see them again.
- In the field, <kbd>Escape</kbd> folds the results away. Press it again to close the panel.
- The **×** in the field clears the field. It shows when the field has words in it.
- The **×** at the top right of the panel closes it.

## What you can search for

| Type this | To find | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A reference | The passage | `John 3:16`, `Jn 3`, `Gen 1:1-2:3` |
| Words | The verses that have all the words | `faith hope charity` |
| A phrase in double quotes | The verses that have that phrase | `"in the beginning"` |
| A Strong's number | The word study of that word | `H2617`, `G25` |
| A Strong's number and a sense | The verses that use the word in that sense | `G4151 sense 7` |
| A word of the original, as it is said | Its word study | `agape`, `chesed`, `shalom`, `logos` |
| Words of the grammar, with a scope | Every word in that form | `aorist imperative in John` |
| Roles of a clause, each with a word | The sentences with those words in those roles | `subject God verb love` |
| A topic | The topic, from Nave's Topical Bible | `prayer`, `forgiveness` |

### Words

A search for words finds the verses that have every word. The verses that have the words as one phrase come first.

A word of four letters or more also finds longer words that start with it. For example, "begin" finds "beginning". A shorter word finds only itself, so "the" does not find "they".

### References

A reference can be a verse, a range, a chapter or a range across chapters. Short book names work, such as "Gen" and "Jn". A reference can cross a chapter, as in `Gen 1:1-2:3`.

### Words of the original

Type a Hebrew or Greek word as it is said, without accents. The usual spellings work: "ch" or "h", "th" or "t", "ow" or "o", "q" or "k". When the spelling fits more than one word, the most used word opens, and the panel offers the others.

When the KJV also has the word, such as "amen", the panel searches the text. It shows the words of the original beside the results.

### Grammar

Type the words of the grammar in any order, then a scope at the end. For example:

- `aorist imperative in John`
- `wayyiqtol in Ruth`
- `G26 accusative`
- `first person plural aorist subjunctive in the gospels`
- `aramaic verb in Daniel`

A scope is a book, a chapter (`Romans 8`), a group (the Law, the Gospels, Paul, the Prophets) or a testament (the OT, the NT).

A word of the grammar also finds the forms that include it. "aorist" also finds the second aorist, and "pronoun" finds every pronoun. But "imperfect" does not find the wayyiqtol. One word alone that the KJV also has, such as "perfect", searches the text.

The answer lists the words that it found most often. Tap one to narrow the search to that word. The answer also shows where the words are, book by book. In each verse, the KJV words that render the words found are underlined.

### Syntax roles

Name the roles of a clause, each with a word: `subject`, `verb`, `object` or `predicate`. Add a scope with "in", if you want.

- `subject God verb love`
- `verb love object world`
- `subject theos verb agapao in John`

A word can be a Strong's number, a word of the original as it is said, or an English word. An English word stands for the words of the original that the KJV often renders by it. The answer is the sentences with a clause that has each word in its role.

### Topics

When a search names a topic of Nave's Topical Bible, the topic shows above the verses, closed. Tap it to open it. Each heading of the topic has its references, and a tap on a reference puts the passage on the table. "See" links go to related topics.

## The results

The summary at the top says what the search found. The panel shows the first 50 verses.

- Tap a result to put it on the table.
- Drag a result out to the side to put it where you want.
- With the keyboard, move to a result with <kbd>Tab</kbd> and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

The words of the passage guide, a commentary or a dictionary entry select as on a web page, so you can copy them. The words of a result do not select, because a drag on a result puts it on the table. To copy some of a result's words, put it on the table, then use text mode. See [Copy](/guide/cards#copy).

### Your notes

When you search for words, your notes that have all of those words show above the results. Notes on this table come first, then notes in each of your saved tables.

- Tap a note on this table to bring it into view.
- Tap a note in a saved table to open that table and show the note. Undo goes back to the table you had before.

Your notes stay on your device. The search of them happens there too.

Some answers have more buttons:

- **Where, chapter by chapter** puts a landscape of the search on the table. See [Study](/guide/study#the-landscape).
- **Mark it on every card** marks the word on every card. See [Tools](/guide/tools#marks).
- Under a passage that you typed, the panel lists the videos of that passage.

## The research trail

The trail is the row of chips under the search field. Each chip is one thing that you looked at, with the icon of its kind.

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Go back | Tap ‹, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> |
| Go forward | Tap ›, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> |
| Go to a step | Tap its chip |
| See the whole trail | Tap **…** |

When you go back to a step, the marks on the cards, the map and the timeline go back with it. A search that you type is one step, not one step for each letter. When you open a thing that is already on the trail, the trail goes to its chip.

The trail is kept with the table. A saved table has its own trail. A step on the trail is not a change for Undo.

## Did you know?

The ✦ at the start of the trail shows a fact about the Bible. The app calculates each fact from its own data. For example: the shortest verse, the longest life, or how the KJV renders a word of the original.

- **Show me** opens the thing that the fact is about.
- **Another** shows the next fact.

When the search panel has nothing to show, it opens on a fact.

## The questions list

When the table has a question note, a **?** button shows on the trail, with the number of questions. Tap it to list the questions: the open ones first, then the answered ones. Tap a question to bring its note into view. See [Notes](/guide/notes#questions).
