# Listen and watch

This page tells you how to hear a passage read aloud, how to hear a word of the original, and how to use the videos and the pictures.

## Read a passage aloud

Tap the speaker button at the top of a passage card. The button turns lapis blue: the card is now in the listening mode. The card reads aloud, verse by verse, and marks the verse that it reads.

In the listening mode:

- Tap a verse to read from that verse.
- Tap the verse that the card reads now to pause. Tap any verse to go on from there.
- Tap the speaker button to stop. The listening mode ends, and taps on the card do what they always do.

The card keeps its place. The next time, the speaker button says "Listen from verse" with the verse where you stopped, and the reading goes on from there. When a reading comes to the end of the card, the listening mode ends, and the next reading starts at the first verse.

Only one card is in the listening mode at a time.

### Who reads

Choose the voice in **Tools**, **Reading**, **Read aloud**, **Voice**:

| Choice | Who reads |
| --- | --- |
| **The app's voice** | The app's own recorded voice, a plain male voice. It reads the KJV and the BSB, where the app has its recording. |
| **LibriVox** | The recording of the whole KJV by Michael Armenta, for LibriVox. It reads the KJV only. |
| **This device** | The voice of this device. |

Where the chosen voice has no recording of a passage, another recording reads it, if there is one. Else this device's voice reads it. When a recording starts, a short message says who reads.

The device's voice also reads the versions that have no recording:

- The BSB, where the app's voice has no recording.
- The Hebrew or the Greek, on **Original**, **Word by word** and **Syntax**, in their modern pronunciation.

When the device's voice says which word it reads, the card marks that word too. A device with no Hebrew or Greek voice says so. A Mac and an iPhone have good voices for both.

### Speed

Choose the speed in **Tools**, **Reading**, **Read aloud**, **Speed**: **0.8×**, **1×**, **1.15×**, **1.3×** or **1.5×**. A recording keeps the same pitch at every speed. Each device keeps its own speed.

### The lock screen

On a phone, a recording shows on the lock screen. Its Play and Pause buttons pause the reading and go on from where it paused.

## Hear a word of the original

- In a word study, tap the speaker button beside the Hebrew or Greek word.
- On a **Word by word** card, tap the transliteration of a word.

The device's Hebrew or Greek voice says the word, in its modern pronunciation. On **Word by word**, each transliteration shows its syllables, with the stressed syllable in bold. The word study also shows Strong's pronunciation.

## Videos

The app has three series of videos:

- **JESUS (1979)**, scene by scene, from the Jesus Film Project.
- **BibleProject**: the overview of each book.
- **The Chosen**, seasons 1 to 5, episode by episode.

### Find a video

- Open the guide of a passage. The **Video** part lists the videos of that passage.
- Or type a reference in the search field. The videos of that passage show under the verses.

Tap a video to put it on the table as a card. The card lists the passages that the video shows. Tap one to put that passage on the table.

### The player

JESUS and BibleProject play on the card, in the app's own player. The player gets nothing from the source of the video until you tap Play. Only one video plays at a time.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Play and Pause | Plays or pauses the video. A tap on the picture does the same. |
| The position bar | Shows where you are in the video. Drag it to move. |
| Speed | Goes to the next speed: 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2 and 0.75 times. |
| **Captions** | Turns the captions on or off, where the video has them. |
| **Mute** | Turns the sound off or on. |
| **Full screen** | Fills the screen with the video, where the browser can. |

A drag on the picture moves the card, as on any card. If a video does not play, the player gives a link to watch it at its source. **Watch at the source**, under the video, opens it on the source's site in a new tab.

Keys in the player:

- <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>K</kbd> plays and pauses.
- <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> go back and forward 10 seconds. On the position bar, they go 5 seconds.
- <kbd>Escape</kbd> leaves the full screen on an iPhone.

### The Chosen

The Chosen cannot play inside another site. Its card has a **Watch on The Chosen** button. Tap it to open the episode on The Chosen's own site, in a new tab, where it is free to watch.

## Pictures

The app has 66 pictures: photographs of the Holy Land from 1898 to 1946, and objects from a museum, such as the Nimrud reliefs. Each picture is linked to the people, places and events that it shows.

- An entry of a person, place or event shows its first picture large, with its caption and credit, and the others small.
- The guide of a passage shows the pictures of the places and people that it names.

Tap a picture to put it on the table as a card. Tap a name under the picture to show that person, place or event in the search panel.
