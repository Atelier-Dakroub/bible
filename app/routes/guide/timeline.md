# The timeline

This page tells you about the timeline strip: its events, the kings' chronologies, the lanes of world history and the note on dates.

## Open the timeline

Tap **Timeline** on the rail. The strip opens beside the rail. Tap **Timeline** again, or the **×** on the strip, to close it. Like the other panels, the strip can float: see [Start here](/guide/start#move-a-panel).

> On a phone, the timeline is closed at first. When it is open, move the strip sideways with a finger.

## Eras and events

The strip is one row of eras. Each era has the same width, whatever its length in years:

1. Creation to the Flood
2. The Flood to the Exodus
3. The Exodus to Saul
4. The kingdoms
5. Exile and return
6. Between the Testaments
7. The Gospels
8. Acts and the letters

Each event of the Bible is a tick on the strip. Tap a tick to show the event in the search panel, with its date and its verses. On a touch screen, a tap between ticks takes the nearest tick.

The ticks of the events that the cards on the table tell are marked. A line joins each card to its events.

A small purple diamond in the margin of a passage card marks a verse where a dated event begins. Tap it to show the event and mark its tick.

The passage guide also lists the events of a passage, with their dates, under **Events, on the timeline**.

## The kings' chronologies

Scholars date the reigns of the kings of Israel and Judah in different ways. The list on the strip chooses whose dates the reigns have:

- **Kings: Jones** (the first choice)
- **Kings: Thiele**
- **Kings: Galil**
- **Kings: Albright**
- **Kings: Kitchen**

A reign at the date of another chronology shows in lapis, and the note on the strip names that chronology. The entry of a reign in the search panel lists the dates of all of them.

## World history

Tap **World** on the strip. Lanes of world history show under the events, on the same axis:

- Empires, kingdoms and periods, in six regions.
- The rulers that the Bible names, such as Sennacherib, Cyrus and the Caesars, as ochre bars.
- Some dated events of the world around the Bible, such as the battle of Qarqar and the fall of Babylon, as purple marks.

Tap a span to show it in the search panel. Its entry lists the events of the Bible that fall inside its years. Tap **World** again to hide the lanes.

## The note on dates

Tap the ⓘ button (**About the dates**) on the strip. A note says whose dates these are:

- The events of the Bible have the dates of Theographic, which follow the chronology of Floyd Nolen Jones, in the tradition of Ussher. For example, creation in 4004 BC and the Exodus in 1491 BC.
- World history has the usual dates of scholars, from Wikidata.

The two can disagree, and the strip shows where. The app names the chronology wherever it shows a date.

## Keys

The ticks are one stop for the <kbd>Tab</kbd> key, and the world spans are another. Use the arrow keys to move from tick to tick, or from span to span. <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> go to the first and the last. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to show the one in focus.
