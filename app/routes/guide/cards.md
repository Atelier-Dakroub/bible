# Cards

This page tells you about a passage card and its buttons, the words on a card, highlights, links between cards, and phrase cards.

## A passage card

A passage card shows a passage of the Bible. Its heading is the reference, such as "John 3:16-18".

These buttons are at the top of the card:

| Button | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ‹ | Before the heading | Goes to the passage before, of the same length. |
| › | After the heading | Goes to the passage after, of the same length. |
| Speaker | At the top | Reads the passage aloud. See [Listen and watch](/guide/listen-watch). |
| Copy | At the top | Copies the words with their reference and version. |
| Sync | At the top | Makes the card step with the other synced cards. |
| Link | At the top | Links the whole card to another card. See [Links between cards](#links-between-cards). |
| **×** | At the top | Takes the card off the table. |

### Versions

Under the heading is a row of version buttons. Tap one to show the passage in that version. The card stays in its place.

| Button | What the card shows |
| --- | --- |
| **KJV** | The King James Version. |
| **BSB** | The Berean Standard Bible. |
| **Both** | The KJV and the BSB side by side, verse by verse. |
| **Original** | The Hebrew (with the Aramaic of Daniel and Ezra), from right to left, or the Greek. |
| **Word by word** | Each word of the original, over its transliteration, its English, its grammar and its Strong's number. |
| **Reverse** | The KJV in its own order, with the word of the original under each word or phrase that renders it. |
| **Syntax** | The Hebrew or the Greek, clause by clause: subject, verb, object and the other parts. |

Some notes on the versions:

- In **Both**, a verse that the BSB leaves out of its main text says "Not in the BSB's main text".
- In **Both**, the **Compare** button sets in bold the words that one translation has and the other does not. It compares the forms of words: "believeth" and "believes" are the same word.
- In **Original** and **Word by word**, the Greek is the text that the KJV translated. A word that the modern critical text does not have has a dotted ochre line under it.
- In **Reverse**, English in a softer ink has no word of the original of its own. It is part of a word (such as "in the"), or the translators supplied it.
- In **Syntax**, each clause is on a row of its own, and each part is a box with its role. A clause inside another clause is indented under it. The Greek of this version is a different edition (Nestle 1904), and the card says so.

Highlights, links, quotes in a note and cards of words work on the words of the KJV and of the BSB. The marks on every card and word studies work on the KJV only.

### Step through the text

Tap ‹ or › beside the heading. The card goes to the passage before or after, of the same length. A chapter goes to the next chapter. A range of verses goes to the next range of as many verses.

**Sync** joins cards. Tap **Sync** on two or more cards. When one synced card steps, the others go to the same passage, each in its own version. For example, put the KJV and the BSB of one chapter side by side, and step through the book.

### Copy

Tap the copy button. The words of the card go to the clipboard, in the version on show, with the reference and the version under them. The button says "Copied".

### The heading

Tap the heading of a passage card. The search panel shows the passage guide: parallels, quotations, cross-references, the people and places it names, and more. See [Study](/guide/study#the-passage-guide). With the keyboard, give the card the focus and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

## Size and the short form

When you make a card smaller than about half size, it shows its short form: the heading, the first words and the number of verses. Make it larger again to read the whole passage.

A passage's heading always stays on one line. In the short form, it uses the short name of its book, such as "1 Cor 13" for 1 Corinthians 13 or "Song 2:1" for Song of Solomon 2:1. At full size, a heading that is too long for the room beside the buttons uses the short name too, such as "2 Cor 6:11". A card in Both is wide, so it keeps the full name. A long reference shows in smaller letters.

## Words on a card

Tap a word on a card:

- A word that names a person, place or group has a dotted underline. A tap shows that person, place or group in the search panel.
- Another word with a Strong's number opens its word study in the search panel.
- A word with no Strong's number, or a tap between words, lists the cross-references of the verse.

See [Study](/guide/study) for what each of these shows.

On **Original**, **Word by word** and **Syntax**, a tap on a Hebrew or Greek word opens its word study. On **Word by word**:

- A tap on the grammar of a word finds every word in that form.
- A tap on the transliteration says the word aloud.

### Marks in the margins

- A small purple diamond after a verse number marks where a dated event begins. Tap it to show the event and its tick on the timeline.
- A lapis quotation mark in the right margin marks a quotation of the Old Testament in the New. Tap it to see the other side.

## Highlights

A highlight takes two hands:

1. Hold the card still with one finger.
2. With a finger of the other hand, touch a word and run over the words.
3. Lift the finger.

With a mouse, hold <kbd>Option</kbd> (or <kbd>Alt</kbd>) and drag over the words.

A highlight belongs to the words, not to the card. Every card that shows those words shows the highlight. The KJV and the BSB have different words, so a highlight in one does not show in the other. A highlight stays in the translation where it starts: beside the KJV, a drag from the KJV into the BSB stops at the end of the KJV.

A highlight has one of three styles: **Filled**, **Underlined** or **Boxed**. Choose the style of the next highlight in **Tools**, **Marks**, **New highlights**.

- To remove a highlight, hold the card and tap a highlighted word.
- Or mark the same words again in the same style.
- To change the style, choose another style, then mark the same words again.

## Links between cards

A link joins two cards, or words on two cards. Every card can have links: passages, notes, people, places, events, videos and the map.

### Link two whole cards

Drag the link button at the top of a card onto another card, with one finger or the mouse. On a small card in its short form, the link button is in the bottom corner, beside the count of verses. Lift it on the other card. A line now joins the two cards.

You can also tap or click the link button. A list of the other cards on the table opens. Choose a card from the list. The list also reaches cards that are off the screen, and it works with the keyboard. "Linked" in the list shows a card that is linked already. Escape closes the list.

A link to a passage is a link to the passage in every translation. The line stays when you change the card from KJV to BSB or to the original. If two cards show the same passage, the line goes to both. When you take the last card of a passage, note or map off the table, its whole-card links go too. Undo brings them back.

### Move linked cards together

Cards that are linked move as a group. A pile of ideas then goes anywhere on the table in one move.

- **With a mouse or a trackpad:** hold Shift and drag a card.
- **With a finger:** hold a card still for half a second, then drag it with the same finger.

When the group starts, a blue ring shows round each card in it, before anything moves. The group is every card that you can reach from the card along links, one step after another: if A links to B and B links to C, all three move. The cards in the group only move: they do not turn or change size. A drag without Shift, or a finger that moves at once, moves one card only. One Undo puts the whole group back.

A link between two piles joins them into one group. To move a pile alone, take that link away first.

### Link words

1. Hold a card and mark words, as for a highlight.
2. Do not lift the marking finger. Drag it out of the card and onto another card.
3. Lift it on a highlight, or on any words of the other card.

The marked words now link to the highlight under your finger. When no highlight is there, they link to the whole card. To quote words in a note instead of linking them, lift them on the note. See [Notes](/guide/notes#quotes).

The lines between cards:

| Line | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Solid curve | A link that you drew. |
| Dashed | The same highlighted words, shown on more than one card. |
| Dotted | A cross-reference from a verse on one card to a verse on another card. |

A link never covers the text. It leaves a card at the side that faces the other card, as a curve, and it goes under any card in its way. A dot on the card's edge shows where a link leaves the card. For a link of words, the dot is level with the words, and a blue bar in the margin marks them.

### Link types

A label beside each link says what the link is. Tap the label to change it, in this order: "link", "quotes", "fulfills", "contrasts", and "link" again. A link with a type has an arrow, from where you started the link to where you dropped it.

The label stands where no card is: beside the dot at one end, or along the curve. When cards are close together and there is no room, the label shows only after you touch one of the two cards.

To remove a link, draw it again. When you remove a highlight, its links go too.

## Phrase cards

Mark words with two hands, then lift the marking finger on the empty table. The marked words become a card of their own, a phrase card. Its words keep their place in the Bible, so its highlights and links show as on any card.

To put marked words into a note as a quote, lift the marking finger on a note. See [Notes](/guide/notes#quotes).

## Other cards

- **A person, place, group or event.** Pull the heading of an entry out of the search panel. Tap the card's heading to show the entry in the panel again. A reference in its text puts that passage beside the card.
- **A picture.** Tap a picture in the search panel. The names under the picture show what it shows.
- **A video.** See [Listen and watch](/guide/listen-watch#videos).
- **The map.** See [The map](/guide/map).
- **The landscape.** See [Study](/guide/study#the-landscape).
