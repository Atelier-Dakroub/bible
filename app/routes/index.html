Bible

A research surface for the text.

  • The table: search the text and pull passages onto an infinite table.
  • Search: the same search, as a page.
  • The reading room: the design system that follows the black one, with its reading settings.
  • Touch test: counts the contact points, checks that the touches land where the screen shows them, and moves a card with one or two fingers.
  • Sources and licenses: where the text, the data, the map and the pictures come from.
